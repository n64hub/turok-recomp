An update to Turok: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux: the stick and the keys reach the picture in less than half the time, and anti-aliasing goes up to 8X.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**What's new:**

- **Much less input lag:** each frame waited most of its time with the pad it read already old, and played with the pad as it was at the end of the frame before it. The game now starts each frame just in time for its turn on the screen, reads the pad at the frame's own start, and shows the frame as soon as it is drawn. Measured from a press of the stick or a key to the picture changing, the median is about 80 ms instead of 180 ms (of which about 70 ms is the measurement's own capture of the window). Where the game runs slower than 30 frames a second, frames start and show as before.
- **MS Anti-Aliasing** goes up to **8X**, where the GPU can take it (greyed out where it can't). 2X stays the default.

**Windows:** unzip `TurokRecompiled-v0.2.10-windows-x64.zip` and run `TurokRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `TurokRecompiled-v0.2.10-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `TurokRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). You need your own ROM of the USA release, revision 1.0 (a big-endian `.z64` of 8 MiB, SHA-1 `40fb0250c095740031278fb1f82b9937a3895e01`): pick it with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**.

**Not checked yet:** the change on Windows (it was measured on the Linux build), the levels past the first one's start, the bosses and long play sessions. Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-recomp/issues), saying where you were and what you did.

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/turok-dinosaur-hunter](https://n64hub.com/turok-dinosaur-hunter/), with the same change (anti-aliasing isn't available in a browser).
