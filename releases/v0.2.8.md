An update to Turok: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux: in a wide window the world is drawn out to the picture's edges, and no piece of the level is drawn out of place for a frame while you move.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Fixed:**

- The level is drawn as far out as the window shows, at any width. Before, it was drawn out to a fixed 21:9 whatever the window: at 32:9 the world stopped short of the picture's edges, and a 4:3 window drew the world out past its sides where nobody sees it.
- While the camera moves, a piece of the level that just came into view, or that the game swapped for a more or less detailed one, is no longer drawn for a frame at the place of one that left: slabs of cliff over the sky, or holes at the picture's edge through to the background.
- The game keeps running when the graphics driver resets, and frees memory the renderer no longer uses.

These come with N64Recomp-browser v0.235.0's renderer and runtime.

**Windows:** unzip `TurokRecompiled-v0.2.8-windows-x64.zip` and run `TurokRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `TurokRecompiled-v0.2.8-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `TurokRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). You need your own ROM of the USA release, revision 1.0 (a big-endian `.z64` of 8 MiB, SHA-1 `40fb0250c095740031278fb1f82b9937a3895e01`): pick it with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**.

**Not checked yet:** the levels past the first one's start, the bosses, saving to and loading from the Controller Pak, long play sessions, and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-recomp/issues), saying where you were and what you did.
