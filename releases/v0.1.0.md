The first release of Turok: Recompiled for Windows and Linux: a native PC port of the N64 *Turok: Dinosaur Hunter* made by static recompilation. It is not an emulator and contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release, revision 1.0 (a big-endian `.z64` of 8 MiB, SHA-1 `40fb0250c095740031278fb1f82b9937a3895e01`). Other regions and revisions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `TurokRecompiled-v0.1.0-windows-x64.zip` and run `TurokRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `TurokRecompiled-v0.1.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `TurokRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**What works:** the legal screen, the logos, the intro and the title menu; a new game into the first level, its fly-over and play: walking, turning, jumping, the bow, the enemies, swimming, and the pause menu. Sound (the game's own audio code, recompiled), and keyboard and gamepad input.

**Enhancements:**

- High resolution at the window's size, with anti-aliasing
- Keyboard and gamepad controls, rebindable

**Not checked yet:** the levels past the first one's start, the bosses, saving to and loading from the Controller Pak, long play sessions, and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Expect the odd thing drawn wrong or a spot that hangs; reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-recomp/issues), saying where you were and what you did.

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/turok-dinosaur-hunter](https://n64hub.com/turok-dinosaur-hunter/). The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-recomp#readme) has how to build it yourself.
