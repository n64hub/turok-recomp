An update to Turok: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux: looking up or down holds where you leave it, and more controllers are mapped.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Fixed:**

- Looking up or down with the right stick or the arrow keys moves the view at once and leaves it where the stick lets go, as in Turok 2 and 3. Before, the view eased towards the stick's angle about a second behind it and sank back level once the stick was let go.

**More controllers:** the controller database is refreshed from SDL's, which maps 32 more pads and updates 17.

**Windows:** unzip `TurokRecompiled-v0.2.9-windows-x64.zip` and run `TurokRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `TurokRecompiled-v0.2.9-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `TurokRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). You need your own ROM of the USA release, revision 1.0 (a big-endian `.z64` of 8 MiB, SHA-1 `40fb0250c095740031278fb1f82b9937a3895e01`): pick it with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**.

**Not checked yet:** the levels past the first one's start, the bosses and long play sessions. Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-recomp/issues), saying where you were and what you did.

The same game plays in a browser at [n64hub.com/turok-dinosaur-hunter](https://n64hub.com/turok-dinosaur-hunter/).
