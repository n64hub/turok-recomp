- **Much less input lag:** each frame waited most of its time with the pad it read already old, and played with the pad as it was at the end of the frame before it. The game now starts each frame just in time for its turn on the screen, reads the pad at the frame's own start, and shows the frame as soon as it is drawn. Measured from a press of the stick or a key to the picture changing, the median is about 70 ms instead of 180 ms (of which about 70 ms is the measurement's own capture of the window). Where the game runs slower than 30 frames a second, frames start and show as before.
- **MS Anti-Aliasing** goes up to **8X**, where the GPU can take it (greyed out where it can't). 2X stays the default.
