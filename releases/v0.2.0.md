An update to Turok: Recompiled, for Windows and Linux: mouse look and a shooter's controls. What's new below is everything since v0.1.0, so it includes what v0.1.1 brought (widescreen, the renderer update).

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**What's new:**

- Mouse look: the mouse turns and looks, and its left button fires. It is on from the first start (General, Mouse Sensitivity, 50%; 0 turns it off and leaves the cursor free); if you played v0.1.0 or v0.1.1, your saved settings have it at 0, so turn it up there
- A shooter's controls, as Turok 2 and 3 have them: WASD to walk and strafe, the mouse or the arrow keys to look, Left Ctrl or the left mouse button to fire, Space to jump, Q and E to change weapon, Tab for the map; on a controller, the two sticks, the right trigger to fire and the bottom face button or the left trigger to jump. All rebindable on the Controls tab
- Up looks up: moving the mouse forward, the up arrow and the right stick pushed up all look up, though the game itself looks down with its stick pushed up
- Widescreen (v0.1.1): in a window wider than 4:3 the level reaches the window's edges and the HUD sits at its sides (Aspect Ratio, on the Graphics settings; Original keeps the 4:3 picture)
- The map (Tab, or the controller's top face button) draws its lines: the level's outline, your arrow and the north pointer, at 4:3 and in widescreen; and opening it no longer crashes the game a few seconds later
- The renderer and runtime are updated to N64Recomp-browser v0.184.0's (v0.1.1)

**Windows:** unzip `TurokRecompiled-v0.2.0-windows-x64.zip` and run `TurokRecompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `TurokRecompiled-v0.2.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `TurokRecompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). You need your own ROM of the USA release, revision 1.0 (a big-endian `.z64` of 8 MiB, SHA-1 `40fb0250c095740031278fb1f82b9937a3895e01`): pick it with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**.

**Not checked yet:** the levels past the first one's start, the bosses, saving to and loading from the Controller Pak, long play sessions, and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/turok-recomp/issues), saying where you were and what you did.
